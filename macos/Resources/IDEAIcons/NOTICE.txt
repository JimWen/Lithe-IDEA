This directory contains selected icon resources from the JetBrains IntelliJ
Platform Community repository and its JetBrains Maven plugin:

https://github.com/JetBrains/intellij-community/tree/master/platform/icons/src
https://github.com/JetBrains/intellij-community/tree/master/plugins/maven/src/main/resources/images

2.0. See LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt for the license text.

toolwindows/toolWindowCargo.svg, toolwindows/toolWindowGo.svg and their dark
variants are original Lithe drawings of outlined R and G letters. They replace
the former IntelliJ Rust Cargo resource and use this repository's license.
LICENSE-MIT.txt is retained for the historical IntelliJ Rust attribution.
